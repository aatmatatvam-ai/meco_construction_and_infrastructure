SHEET 01 / 06 — COMPANY PROFILE

About Meco

Meco Construction & Infratech Private Limited is an active infrastructure and building-completion company, incorporated in September 2021. The company takes on major infrastructure and construction contracts, with a key operating branch in Manvi, a taluk in Raichur district, Karnataka.

Our work spans the full contracting cycle — from site survey and mobilization through execution and handover — on road, highway and building works across the region.

M. Eranna

Founder & Managing Director

M. Eranna is the Founder & Managing Director of Meco Construction & Infratech Private Limited. He is an Indian contractor and business owner whose firm specializes in building completion, construction and infrastructure development, operating out of the company's branch office on Raichur Road, Manvi, Karnataka, since the company's incorporation in September 2021.

Beyond the company, he founded and manages the M. Eranna Foundation, based in Manvi, which carries out local social work and free public services in the region.
